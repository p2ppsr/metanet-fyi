# Commercial Readiness Framework record

Reviewed: 2026-09-03

## Product thesis

Metanet.fyi is a free educational and ecosystem-onramp surface. Its commercial value is indirect but concrete: lower comprehension cost, better routing into maintained standards and implementations, stronger continuity practices, and measurable movement from curiosity into publishing or building paths.

## Audience and job

- Curious ecosystem participant: understand overlays without first learning internal vocabulary.
- On-chain publisher or community: assess whether records can outlive the original app.
- Developer: move from concept to current specifications and maintained packages.
- Operator or protocol steward: identify synchronization, completeness, portability, abuse, privacy, and succession obligations.

## Differentiation

The product does not compete with BRCs, package READMEs, or official SDK documentation. It supplies the missing editorial bridge: real-world question → visual mental model → important correction → primary source → role-specific action.

## Value and conversion

The first content pillar is overlays. Primary success events are guide starts, pathway selections, source referrals, and survival-checklist engagement. No payment or account conversion is appropriate for the initial product. The call to action is informed ecosystem participation.

## Trust, risk, and policy

- Important claims route to primary sources; current, historical, archived, and aspirational resources are labeled.
- The project is explicit that it is independent and not an official BSV Association publication.
- There are no accounts, wallets, payments, user-submitted content, or privileged actions.
- Measurement is bounded to coarse interaction signals and disabled for GPC/DNT.
- Content and source data are portable under CC BY 4.0; code is MIT.

## Delivery and operations

- Node 22 server-side rendering with semantic HTML and progressive enhancement.
- No runtime npm dependencies, remote fonts, or blocking client framework.
- Per-route canonical, description, Open Graph, Twitter Card, and schema.org metadata.
- Sitemap, robots, `llms.txt`, machine-readable source atlas, security headers, compression, ETags, and health endpoint.
- Linux/amd64 image built by GitHub Actions on Evans Creek and pinned in Kubernetes by source SHA.
- Two replicas, disruption budget, topology spread, readiness/liveness checks, TLS, DNS, and rollback owned in `network-ops`.
- Automated unit, responsive, accessibility, and Lighthouse gates; ProofRun journeys provide human-intent evidence.

## Adoption loop

1. A reader enters through a high-specificity concept or recovery question.
2. The visual guide establishes a correct mental model and exposes a useful correction.
3. Role-based calls to action route to the recovery test, build path, or sources.
4. Aggregate signals reveal where readers progress or stop.
5. Editorial work improves weak transitions and adds guides where the source atlas shows recurring conceptual gaps.

## Release gate

Growth-ready status requires a healthy public deployment, recent passing CI and Lighthouse evidence, current ProofRun flow definitions and passing run records, an operational dossier, DNS/TLS monitoring, adoption telemetry, and a tested rollback path.
